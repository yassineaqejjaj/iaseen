# Blog editorial plan: SEO and GEO

Goal: be the cited answer for "agentic AI in the enterprise" questions, with Yassine Aqejjaj as a named, verifiable author (Head of AI Factory, Devoteam; builder of NOVA, ORBIT, FORGE).

## Format rules for every article (what search and AI engines quote)

1. **Answer first.** The first paragraph is a 40 to 60 word definition or direct answer to the title question.
2. **1,500 to 2,200 words**, with H2s written as questions people ask.
3. **One table** (comparison, checklist or metrics) and **one diagram**: tables are the most quoted format.
4. **One section "What I measured / what I saw"** with a real, validated figure and its source. No invented numbers.
5. **FAQ block of 4 to 5 questions** with short answers (also in `FAQPage` JSON-LD).
6. **Links:** the related case study, the related GitHub repo, two sibling articles.
7. **Dates:** `datePublished` and a visible "Updated on" line, refreshed every quarter.
8. **Entity consistency:** always "Yassine Aqejjaj, Head of AI Factory at Devoteam", the same bio, the same LinkedIn and GitHub links.
9. **Three languages:** English is the source. French and Arabic are written for their own queries, not translated word for word.
10. **Confidentiality:** defence and client-confidential work only in public or client-approved terms (no Thales internals).
